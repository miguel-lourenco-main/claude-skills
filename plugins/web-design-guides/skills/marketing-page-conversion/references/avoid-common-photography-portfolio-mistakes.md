## Use when
Building or auditing an image-heavy portfolio (photography, but the curation/perf/contact rules generalize to any visual-work portfolio).

## Rules
- Curate to 20-30 exceptional images total; below ~10 reads as inexperience, above ~30 dilutes impact (per Magnum Photos portfolio-review guidance, unsourced elsewhere).
- A portfolio is only as strong as its weakest image — cut anything that doesn't represent the work you want more of.
- Export images at consistent technical specs: 1500px on the long edge, 90%+ JPEG quality (higher for skies/gradients, to avoid banding), sRGB color space.
- Editing-style consistency across the whole set matters as much as per-image quality — clients hire based on the style they see, and inconsistent editing makes outcomes unpredictable to them.
- Never bury contact info: use a four-location strategy — persistent header link, inline CTA every 3-5 projects, prominent homepage contact block (above the fold), and a footer contact block on every page. Add click-to-call on mobile.
- Page load under 3 seconds on mobile is a hard threshold: bounce probability rises 32% going from 1s to 3s, and 53% of mobile visitors abandon past 3s (per Google research). Over 60% of traffic is mobile (per StatCounter).
- Specialize or segment: mixed-genre portfolios read as less expert and increase price sensitivity; separate galleries/sections per specialty preserve both breadth and clarity.
- Refresh quarterly (every 3-6 months): replace the 3-5 weakest images each cycle; cut dated processing styles and anything you wouldn't proudly show today.

## Patterns (name — when to use — when to avoid)
- Four-location contact placement — always, for any portfolio meant to generate inquiries — no real avoid case.
- Separate galleries per specialty — when you genuinely work multiple niches — avoid if it fragments a small body of work into too-thin sections.

## Anti-patterns
- Uploading every project ever made ("photo dump") instead of curating.
- Contact info only in a footer with no other path.
- Mixed editing styles across the gallery.
- Letting the portfolio go stale (dated trends signal you're not actively working).

## Checklist (only if supported)
- 20-30 images, each reviewed at 100% zoom for sharpness/compression artifacts.
- Contact link present in header, mid-scroll CTA, homepage, and footer.
- Mobile load tested under 3 seconds.
- Quarterly review scheduled to swap weakest 3-5 images.
