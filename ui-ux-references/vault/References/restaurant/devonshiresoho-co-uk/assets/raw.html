Frequently
Asked
Questions

When will your bookings
open further?

We open our a la carte bookings every Thursday at 10.30am for 3 weeks in advance. If there is a particular date you are interested in, click it on the booking widget and it'll tell you the relevant Thursday to be on the website to make the booking. For larger bookings, please enquire via the feasting option.


Do you do Sunday Roasts?

Of course! Please see our menus page for our Sunday Lunch menu.
Please note, this is the only menu we serve for lunch on Sunday, 12pm to 4pm.
For dinner service on Sunday, from 4.15pm, we switch back to our a la carte menu.


Can I book the restaurant
for a large table?

Tables of 8 - 14 will be enjoying our 'feasting style' menu. Please enquire via the Feasting option on the booking widget and the team will be in touch. Feasting enquiries can be made ahead of a la carte bookings opening 3 weeks in advance. Unfortunately we are unable to accommodate groups of this size on our a la carte or set menu.


Can I make a booking on
the roof terrace?

Our roof terrace is reserved for walk-ins only. If it's a sunny day, it's worth popping in to see if we have space. If you have a restaurant reservation and would like to sit on the roof,
please ask the hosts if this is possible when checking in.


When is your set menu available?

We serve our set menu all day in the restaurant Monday - Saturday and for dinner service on Sunday, subject to availability.


Do we have space for prams?

If you're bringing a pram, please let us know in the booking notes so that the host team can accommodate you on a suitable table. We do need to know this in advance.


Do you take bookings in the pub?

No, our downstairs pub is walk-in only. All bookings are for our restaurant. We do not take any drinks-only / bar snacks reservations.


Do you do bar snacks?

Yes!
Our menu is on the
back of our beer mats.


Do you allow dogs?

Dogs are allowed in the pub.
Small, well-behaved dogs are also welcome in the restaurant.


Can we work from the pub?

We don't allow laptops or loud phone calls in the pub.


Is there disabled access?

Yes, we have step free access to the pub and a
lift to access the restaurant and the accessible toilet. For restaurant bookings, please let us know in advance to accommodate you on a suitable table.


Do you do private hire?

Yes! Please take a look at our private hire brochure and enquire via our BOOK NOW button. For any detailed questions, please email privateevents@devonshiresoho.co.uk.


Do you do gift vouchers?

Yes we do! Please see the gift vouchers section above.


Do you have a waiting list?

We currently aren't running waiting lists for tables.
We accept walk ins inside and on the roof terrace, subject to availability.


Are children allowed in the restaurant and the pub?

Children are very welcome in the restaurant at any time.
Unfortunately, we are unable to welcome children in the pub
after 5pm on weekdays or at any time over the weekend.


Do you have a dress code?

No!


Do you do corkage?

We don't do corkage here.


Do you cater for vegans?

Unfortunately we have a limited menu and whilst
we have a number of delicious vegetarian dishes,
we sadly have very little that is fully vegan.
