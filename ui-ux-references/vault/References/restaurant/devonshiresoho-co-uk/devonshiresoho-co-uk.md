## Strengths
- Dual-panel hero does the IA work instantly — visitors self-select Public House vs Restaurant before any scroll, mirroring the venue's actual two-concept layout with zero explanatory copy
- Photography is the brand: dim, candlelit, high-warmth food/exterior shots establish a premium gastropub mood that no amount of typography could replicate
- Disciplined, tiny accent palette (bottle green, antique gold, Guinness red) reads unmistakably 'British pub' and ties the CTA, panel labels, and modal into one system
- Welcome modal pre-empts the #1 confusion (how do I book?) by directing users to the green Book Now button before they hunt for it — proactive UX onboarding rare on restaurant sites
- Restraint in chrome: transparent nav, centered serif wordmark with 'SOHO SINCE 1793' provenance line, and minimal links keep focus on imagery and the single booking action
- Menus are honest, fast PDF links (no bloated digital menu builder) — appropriate, low-friction, and keeps the marketing site light (single 544-line hand-coded page)

## Weaknesses
- The welcome modal overlaps and obscures the center of the hero on load, partially hiding the wordmark and forcing a dismissal before the brand image fully reads
- All-caps Avenir body copy in the modal is harder to scan and the gold-on-green contrast is borderline for accessibility at small sizes
- Hand-coded single-page structure with PDF-only menus means no SEO-indexable menu content and limited responsive flexibility

## Notable patterns
- Clickable dual-panel hero with overlaid all-caps green label chips ('PUBLIC HOUSE' / 'RESTAURANT') that splits one venue into two navigable identities
- Welcome/onboarding modal that explicitly points to the primary CTA ('press the BOOK NOW button') — turns a popup from annoyance into a wayfinding aid
- Provenance microcopy baked into the logo lockup ('SOHO SINCE 1793') and a low-center italic address tag ('17 Denman St') to sell legitimacy and place
- Single high-contrast red CTA isolated on a neutral taupe band so the one action you can take is unmissable against an otherwise green/gold scheme
- Soft cream light-bleed glow at the page's bottom edge mimicking candle/warm-lamp lighting, reinforcing the 'cosy at night' mood as a reusable atmospheric flourish
- Menus exposed as a flat list of labeled PDF links — pragmatic, fast, and avoids over-engineering a digital menu

## When to use
Use for a heritage / two-concept hospitality venue (pub + dining room, or bar + restaurant) that leans on atmospheric photography and wants visitors to pick a 'side of the house' and book in one tap.
